<h2 style="margin: 60px 0px 10px;">About Me</h2>

<div class="justify-text">
I am a Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://ece.gatech.edu/">School of Electrical and Computer Engineering</a> at the <a href="https://www.gatech.edu/">Georgia Institute of Technology</a> in Atlanta, advised by <a href="https://greenlab.ece.gatech.edu/">Dr. Saibal Mukhopadhyay</a> in the GREEN Lab. I develop <strong>adaptive perception and closed-loop sensor control</strong> for robots with limited compute and memory. My work combines <strong>radar, cameras, LiDAR, and event-based sensing</strong> with efficient learning models deployed on <strong>Intel NPUs, NVIDIA Jetson, and FPGAs</strong>, and integrates them into real-time perception, planning, and navigation on <strong>Boston Dynamics Spot</strong> and <strong>LoCoBot</strong>.
</div>

<div class="justify-text">
My research spans <strong>compute-adaptive state-space models</strong> and transformers for streaming sensor data, <strong>multimodal sensor fusion</strong> (radar–camera and radar–event), <strong>generative radar world models</strong> for Real2Sim2Real robot learning, <strong>one-step generative models</strong>, <strong>analog compute-in-memory</strong> for LLMs, and <strong>quantum sensing</strong>. My work appears at <a href="https://cvpr.thecvf.com/">CVPR</a> (Highlight), <a href="https://neurips.cc/">NeurIPS</a>, <a href="https://wacv.thecvf.com/">WACV</a> (Oral), <a href="https://qce.quantum.ieee.org/">IEEE QCE</a>, <strong>IEEE Transactions on Radar Systems</strong>, <a href="https://www.igarss.org/">IGARSS</a>, and <a href="https://ieee-ssci.org/">IEEE SSCI</a>.
</div>

<div class="justify-text" style="margin-top: 1em;">
<strong style="color:#e74d3c; font-weight:600">Looking ahead, my research will focus on scalable multimodal perception for autonomous systems, efficient neural architectures for real-time edge deployment, and co-design methodologies that unify hardware and software for next-generation intelligent sensing.</strong>
</div>
